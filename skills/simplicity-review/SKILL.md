---
name: simplicity-review
description: Review a design or proposal for over-engineering and articulate the simpler alternative, optionally as a friendly peer-review message. Use whenever the user wants a sanity check on a design, suspects something is over-built, is reviewing a colleague's proposal, or asks "is this too complex?" / "how would you simplify this?".
---

Apply simplicity-first rigorously but kindly.

1. **Restate the design** in one paragraph so the author knows you understood it.
2. **Name the complexity** — list each piece of machinery and ask "what concrete requirement forces this?" Flag anything justified only by hypothetical future needs.
3. **Propose the simpler alternative** — the smallest design that meets the *actual* stated requirements. Show what it drops and why that's safe.
4. **Honest tradeoffs** — where the simpler version genuinely loses something, say so; don't pretend simpler is free.
5. **Optional: a peer-review message** — if asked, draft a collaborative, non-dismissive message to the author. Lead with what works, frame the simplification as a question/option, never as a verdict.

Tone: a respected colleague offering a second opinion, not a gatekeeper.
