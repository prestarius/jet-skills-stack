---
name: interview-guide
description: Build a structured technical interview guide with a scoring rubric and an evaluation template, segmented by audience (e.g. engineer vs architect). Use whenever the user is preparing to interview or verify a candidate, asks for interview questions, a screening guide, or a candidate evaluation form.
---

Produce a guide with:
1. **Role context** — the competencies the role actually needs (pull from the JD/conversation).
2. **Competency areas** — group questions by area (e.g. system design, language depth, AI-in-SDLC, collaboration).
3. **Calibrated questions** — per area, 2–4 questions ordered easy → hard. For each: a model answer / what "good" sounds like, and concrete **red flags**.
4. **Scoring rubric** — 1–5 per competency with anchored descriptions for 1, 3, and 5.
5. **Evaluation template** — a fill-in block (scores per competency, evidence notes, overall recommendation: Strong Hire / Hire / No Hire).

If the audience is mixed (engineers AND architects), produce two question tracks; architects get more design/tradeoff/governance depth, engineers get more hands-on/implementation depth. Output Markdown only.
